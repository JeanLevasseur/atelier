# Atelier

Atelier is my digital workspace.

Its purpose is not to accumulate tools, but to build a coherent environment where deep intellectual work can flourish.

Architectural decisions are documented as Architecture Decision Records (ADRs)[^1] to preserve their context and rationale.

The environment evolves incrementally, guided by observed friction rather than trends.

## Language

The repository is written in English to make Atelier accessible to a broader audience.

Discussions, drafts, and design conversations may still take place in French.

[^1]: See the `architecture-decision-records/` directory.
